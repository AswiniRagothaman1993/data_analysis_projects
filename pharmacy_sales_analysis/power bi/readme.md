Key Business Findings:

• May sales increased 6.38%, primarily associated with a higher average bill value despite almost unchanged bill volume.
• June sales declined 7.87% despite a slight increase in bill volume, associated with an 8.44% decline in average bill value.
• Thursday recorded the strongest individual-day performance, supported by relatively high bill volume and the highest average bill value.
• Friday recorded the weakest individual-day performance, with the lowest bill volume and average bill value.
• The 5% GST slab accounted for 94.73% of sales contribution, making it the dominant GST category.
• Sales showed positive relationships with both bill volume and average bill value.
• Pharmacy reporting practices combine selected Sundays and public holidays into prior reporting dates, which was incorporated into the analysis.
